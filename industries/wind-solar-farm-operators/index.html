Industry

Wind & Solar Farm Operators

Renewable energy has a timing problem. The sun and wind deliver when they deliver, often when prices are low, sometimes when the grid is too congested to take it all

How Sparq fits

Curtailment means giving away production you can't sell, and a bare connection point leaves real revenue on the table. Energy storage fixes the timing.

  • Sparq Capitalizer turns a co-located battery into a revenue engine: store cheap or would-be-curtailed energy, sell it when prices climb, and earn from ancillary and support services such as frequency regulation and local flexibility markets.

  • Sparq Controller smooths output and lifts self-consumption.

Case study: Sparq engineered and deployed a complete 10-foot container BESS of 375 kW / 430 kWh (30 battery modules) for Kode Solenergi in Gothenburg, with the system generating revenue through local ancillary services on the Swedish market.

What you get

  • Static Transfer Switch ensures seamless power continuity - your systems never know the grid failed.

  • Replace costly, unreliable diesel UPS with a maintenance-free, emission-free battery system.

  • When not in backup mode, participate in frequency regulation markets and generate returns.

The system

  1. 01From around 375 kW / 430 kWh for a single site up to multi-MW for utility-scale farms
  2. 02Containerized LFP systems
  3. 03Sinexcel PCS
  4. 04Integrated HVAC
  5. 05PFAS-free fire suppression
  6. 06EU-made Sparq EMS managing market participation
