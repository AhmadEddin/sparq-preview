Industry

Commercial & industrial (C&I)

Whatever you make or sell, the electricity bill tells the same story: demand charges you can't quite predict, prices that swing by the hour, and a grid connection that suddenly limits your next expansion or that new production line.

How Sparq fits

Add tightening sustainability requirements and volatile markets, and energy shifts from a background cost to a board-level question. Sparq gives you control over all of it.

  • Sparq Analyzer shows exactly where and when your energy money goes; Sparq Optimizer then acts on it automatically, shifting load and dispatching the battery to cut cost without touching operations.

  • Sparq Controller flattens demand peaks and banks cheap energy for expensive hours, and lets you add machinery or a production line behind an existing connection instead of paying to enlarge it.

  • Sparq Capitalizer turns spare capacity into a second income through balancing and flexibility markets.

  • Sparq Stabilizer protects sensitive processes and IT from the outages and voltage dips that scrap a batch or halt a line.

It's the full toolbox, deployed in whatever combination your site rewards most, and available through flexible financing and leasing so the numbers work from day one.

What you get

  • Seamless continuity

    Static Transfer Switch ensures seamless power continuity - your systems never know the grid failed.

  • No more diesel

    Replace costly, unreliable diesel UPS with a maintenance-free, emission-free battery system.

  • Standby to revenue

    When not in backup mode, participate in frequency regulation markets and generate returns.

The system

  1. 01From 100 kW / kWh to several MW / MWh, matched to your load profile and tariff.
  2. 02LFP systems delivered in container, cabinet or technical building.
  3. 03Sinexcel power conversion
  4. 04Integrated HVAC cooling
  5. 05PFAS-free EN 54 fire suppression
  6. 06Sparq EMS, with grid connection and monitoring built to data-center reliability standards.

Relevant packages

Suitable packages: Controller or Capitalizer as the core, with Stabilizer added wherever uptime is non-negotiable, supported by Analyzer and Optimizer from day one.
