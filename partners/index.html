Partners

Our partners.

No single company builds the energy transition alone. Sparq brings the battery storage and energy management software, our partners bring deep expertise in their own domain, from charging infrastructure and fleet electrification to distributed data infrastructure and energy trading. Together, we deliver solutions that go further than either of us could build on our own. Here are some of the partnerships putting that to work.

Partnership

Siemens e-mobility

Smart Partnership for Future Mobility

SPARQ partners with Siemens to help businesses build smarter and more future-ready e-mobility infrastructure. By combining Siemens' trusted charging technologies with SPARQ's intelligent energy optimization solutions, we create efficient, scalable and seamless EV charging experiences for commercial and industrial environments.

Flagship project: 10ft BESS for Älghult fossil fuel-free mobility hub →

Partnership

PVESS

Driving Smarter Fleet Electrification

SPARQ partners with PVESS to support the transition toward cleaner and smarter transport operations. Together, we helped enable the electrification of a waste management fleet through integrated charging infrastructure and intelligent charging, creating a more efficient, scalable and sustainable fleet operation.

Flagship project: 30ft BESS Container for EV fleet electrification →

Sparq x Insane Infrastructure

Insane Infrastructure

Building the next level of energy & data infrastructure, together

Insane Infrastructure is building a network of distributed, smaller data centers, spreading capacity across many local sites instead of a few massive ones. Sparq provides the battery energy storage that keeps them running, supplying clean backup power so operations continue seamlessly even through a grid outage.

We are building the next level energy & data infrastructure together with Insane Infrastructure, in a time where a few big trillion-dollar companies are building the opposite. Probably insane, but we want everyone to be independent rather than stuck under the rules of a few.

Sparq x Green Voltis

Green Voltis

Front-of-meter trading and ancillary services

Sparq delivers the battery storage and the Sparq EMS on site. Green Voltis takes it to market: its AI-native platform trades and optimizes battery storage in front of the meter, bidding across the day-ahead, intraday and ancillary service markets in real time.

Green Voltis continuously forecasts the markets and allocates the battery's capacity to where it creates the most value, while every dispatch respects state of charge, temperature and warranty limits, protecting the battery's long-term health.

The partnership started with four operational sites, with Green Voltis managing the assets and their performance.
