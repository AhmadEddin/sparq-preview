From our CEO

As part of the CEBON Group, we’ve always been guided by our mission: to build brands that truly make an impact on people’s lives. That same drive led us to explore a new field, where a passion for innovation and the dream of autonomy gave rise to Sparq Technology.

What started as a humble startup with much to learn has grown into a company with its very own in-house R&D team. This journey of growth has only been possible thanks to our shared commitment to shaping a better tomorrow.

And while we’re serious about what we do, we never forget to have fun along the way. Because, in my view, that’s a key ingredient in building strong, sustainable brands.

Here’s to many more exciting years ahead!

Mathias Lindberg, CEO

CEBON Group

Sparq Technology is part of CEBON Group, based in Gothenburg, Sweden. Founded in 1986 by three high school friends and celebrating its 40th anniversary in 2026, CEBON develops and grows brands and solutions within energy, lighting, and fire safety, built on experience, partnerships, and a shared commitment to long-term value.
