What a BESS + EMS can do

Applications

Two sides of one meter: the grid on the left, your site on the right.

Front-of-the-Meter (FTM)

Grid-side / utility-scale, mostly revenue and grid-service driven:

  • Energy arbitrageBuy/store energy when wholesale prices are low, discharge/sell when high (day-ahead and intraday trading)
  • Frequency Containment Reserves (FCR)Fast automatic response to keep grid frequency stable (e.g. FCR-N, FCR-D up/down)
  • Frequency Restoration Reserves (FRR)Automatic (aFRR) and manual (mFRR) balancing services
  • Fast frequency response (FFR) / synthetic inertiaSub-second injection to stabilize frequency on low-inertia grids
  • Voltage support / reactive power (Q) regulationHold voltage within limits locally
  • Capacity market / firm capacityBeing available as guaranteed capacity when the system needs it
  • Reserve & ancillary services (stödtjänster)The broader basket of TSO / DSO support products
  • Renewable firming & smoothingStabilize variable solar/wind output, reduce ramp rates
  • Curtailment reduction / renewable time-shiftingCapture would-be-wasted generation and release it later
  • Congestion management & grid-investment deferralRelieve constrained lines and postpone transmission/distribution upgrades
  • Local flexibility marketsSell flexibility to the DSO where the grid is tight
  • Black startRestart parts of the grid after an outage
  • Peaker replacementCover system peaks instead of fossil peaking plants
Meter

Behind-the-Meter (BTM)

Customer-side, mostly cost savings and resilience:

  • Peak shaving / demand-charge reductionCut the expensive kW peaks on your bill
  • Load shifting / time-of-use optimizationMove consumption to cheaper hours
  • Self-consumption optimizationStore your own solar (or off-peak energy) for use after dark
  • Backup power / UPS / reserve power (reservkraft)Ride through outages, protect critical loads
  • Grid-capacity reliefAdd load, machines or chargers behind an existing connection instead of paying to upgrade it
  • EV charging bufferingDeliver fast charging without a grid reinforcement (Sparq SuperCharger / MobileCharger)
  • Power qualityVoltage support, flicker and harmonic mitigation, protection from dips/sags
  • Genset / diesel replacement or hybridizationCut fuel, noise and emissions on weak-grid or off-grid sites
  • Off-grid / island operationRun fully independent of the grid where needed
  • Phase balancingEven out unbalanced three-phase loads
  • Energy communities & shared storagePool storage across buildings or members
