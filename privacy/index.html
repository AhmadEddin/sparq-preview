Who we are

This website (sparqtechnology.com) is operated by Sparq Technology AB (org.nr 559403-8811), Sörredsvägen 113, 418 78 Göteborg, Sweden. We are the data controller for personal data collected through this site.

What we collect and why

When you use our contact form, we collect the name, email address, and message you provide. We use this solely to respond to your enquiry. We do not use this information for marketing unless you separately ask us to.

We ask for your consent through a cookie banner before setting any non-essential cookies. Nothing below is loaded or set unless you accept analytics cookies. You can change your choice at any time using the cookie icon in the bottom-left corner of the page; rejecting analytics stops the tracking and removes these cookies.

If you accept analytics cookies, we use two tools to understand how the site is used:

  • Our own anonymous visit counter, which sets a single first-party cookie (sparq_vid) for up to one year. It contains no name, email address or cross-site identifier.
  • Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Limited, which sets the cookies _ga and _ga_T0N00R9S26 for up to two years. Google Analytics collects information such as the pages you visit, how long you stay, your approximate location (country/city), device and browser type, and how you arrived at the site. Google may process this data outside the EU/EEA, including in the United States, under the EU, US Data Privacy Framework and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We use Google Analytics only for statistics; advertising features are switched off. Read more in Google's privacy policy.

Legal basis for processing

We process contact form submissions based on your consent, given when you voluntarily submit the form, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

We use analytics cookies, including Google Analytics, only on the basis of your consent given in the cookie banner, which you can withdraw at any time.

How long we keep it

We retain contact form submissions for 3 years from the date of submission, after which they are deleted.

Who we share it with

We use third-party service providers to host this website and store data on our behalf. Where these providers process data outside the EU/EEA, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell personal data to any third party.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data, to restrict or object to our processing of it, and to receive a copy of it in a portable format. You can also withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@sparqtechnology.com or +46 (0)31-799 16 00.

If you believe we have not handled your data properly, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY).

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.
