Sparq Stabilizer

Keep your critical infrastructure running.

SPARQ Stabilizer combines Battery storage, Intelligent energy management, Smart Transfer Switch to provide seamless transition to backup power when needed.

Contact Sparq→ Product sheet↓

The package

Sparq Stabilizer delivers uninterrupted power supply (UPS) for critical infrastructure, hospitals, data centres, telecom networks, and emergency services.

Combining a high-capacity BESS with a Static Transfer Switch, it provides seamless failover in milliseconds, with zero reliance on diesel backup.

Who it is for

Hospitals, data centres, telecom operators, utilities, water treatment facilities, and any organisation where a power outage means operational failure, safety risk, or significant financial loss.

What it does for you

  1. 01

    Seamless continuity

    Static Transfer Switch ensures seamless power continuity - your systems never know the grid failed.

  2. 02

    No more diesel

    Replace costly, unreliable diesel UPS with a maintenance-free, emission-free battery system.

  3. 03

    Standby to revenue

    When not in backup mode, participate in frequency regulation markets and generate returns.
