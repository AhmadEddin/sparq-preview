Sparq Mobilecharger

EV Charging delivered to where your EVs go.

SPARQ MobileCharger combines Mobile battery storage, Intelligent energy management, EV charging station in an all-in-one mobile unit to deliver clean, reliable power where it is needed.

Contact Sparq→ Product sheet↓

The package

Sparq Mobilecharger is a fully mobile Battery Energy Storage System that travels where your operations do. Mounted on a standard truck, it delivers clean, reliable power for construction sites, outdoor events, and remote operations, silently replacing diesel generators and eliminating fuel costs.

Who it is for

Construction companies, infrastructure contractors, event organisers, rental businesses, and any operation requiring temporary off-grid or remote-site power.

What it does for you

  1. 01

    Eliminate diesel on-site

    Replace generators with clean, silent battery power - cut fuel costs and meet emissions targets.

  2. 02

    Power any site in hours

    Deploy to a new location in hours, not months. No grid application, no waiting.

  3. 03

    1 asset, many projects

    Move your battery between sites as projects evolve - maximise utilisation of your investment.

What is included

Key components

  1. 01Mobile BESS (500 kWh to 1 MWh, LFP chemistry)
  2. 02Truck-mounted: fully transportable
  3. 03Optional EV charging outlet (Siemens Sicharge D EV charger, up to 400 kW DC)
  4. 04Optional 230V transformer
  5. 05Powerlock connectors for rapid site connection
  6. 06Sparq EMS for remote monitoring and control
