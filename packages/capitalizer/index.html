Sparq Capitalizer

Turn your energy assets into revenue engines.

SPARQ Capitalizer combines Battery storage, Intelligent energy management, Market aggregation to unlock new recurring revenue streams.

Contact Sparq→ Product sheet↓

The package

Sparq Capitalizer is an energy storage system built to actively generate revenue from European energy markets through Front-Of-The-Meter (FTM) services. By participating in frequency regulation (FCR, aFRR), capacity markets, and energy arbitrage, your battery works for you around the clock, turning a capital asset into a consistent income stream.

Who it is for

Investors, financial institutions (SPVs), energy companies, and large industrial players looking to monetise battery assets through European grid services and wholesale energy markets.

What it does for you

  1. 01

    Generate revenue 24/7

    Earn from frequency regulation markets even when your facility is idle or off-peak.

  2. 02

    Diversify revenue

    Combine FCR and arbitrage with behind-the-meter services for maximum return on your battery investment.

  3. 03

    Easy market access

    Sparq EMS handles market optimisation automatically - no energy trading expertise required.
