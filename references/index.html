  1. Sparq SPV

    Sparq's own Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV), a dedicated commercial structure that delivers ancillary services to the Swedish grid, including mFRR (manual Frequency Restoration Reserve). By aggregating flexible battery capacity behind the SPV, Sparq helps balance supply and demand on the grid while building a stable, long-term revenue stream from the ancillary services market.

    Location Skällinge, SwedenCommissioned September 2026

    Capitalizer

  2. Charging infrastructure for Verdis in Stockholm

    Verdis needed to charge a fleet of 20+ electric waste-collection trucks every night, but the site's grid connection wasn't sufficient to support it. Together with PVESS, Sparq delivered a containerized BESS to buffer the load, enabling reliable overnight charging for the whole fleet without a costly grid upgrade.

    Location Stockholm, SwedenCommissioned May 2026

    SuperchargerTransport & logistics

  10. Large-scale BESS

    A 20 foot container with air cooling system of 750kW-860kWh. Designed for ancillary services to maintain grid stability and reliability. The system is built with battery modules inside of the container which enables capacity expansion without a full system replacement, reducing long-term costs for the end-client.

    Location SwedenDate December 2025

    Capitalizer

  11. BESS in Båstad

    A robust 375kW/430kWh system, designed to connect seamlessly to the local flex market in Båstad. Containerized battery energy storage system for outdoor use, strategically positioned next to a large grocery store. Built of 30 battery modules with an Integrated Swedish EMS system from Voltic. The scalable design allows for future capacity expansion without the replacement of the whole system.

    Location SwedenDate July 2025

    CapitalizerCommercial & industrial (C&I)

  15. BESS in Örnsköldsvik
