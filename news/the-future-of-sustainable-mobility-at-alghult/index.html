The future of sustainable mobility… at Älghult!

SuperchargerTransport & logistics

An all integrated clean mobility hub.. in Älghult!

On April 23, we flipped the switch on something we've been working toward for a long time: a fully integrated energy hub where Energy storage systems, solar panels, fast chargers, wind power and hydrogen collaborate.

Together with Siemens E-mobility, we've developed a solution that combines high-power DC charging, battery energy storage, and our own in-house EMS.

Faster, more reliable charging for heavy transport without putting unnecessary strain on the grid.

With over 100 people on site, the day was filled with sharp discussions, honest perspectives on the future of energy supply, and most importantly, a shared sense that real progress happens when technology meets collaboration.

We're proud to contribute with what we do best. And even more proud to do it alongside partners who are just as committed to accelerating the transition.

Thank you to everyone who joined us on that day! This is only the beginning.

In case you missed out on this landmark event, contact us now and know what you missed out on!
