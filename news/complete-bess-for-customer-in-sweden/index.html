10 October 2023

BESS for customer in Sweden

One of Sparq's most recent projects involved a client request for a Battery Energy Storage System (BESS) tailored for agricultural use. The client's specifications were for a system with a capacity of 400 kW to 430 kWh, which included a fire-fighting system, Battery Management System (BMS), and a Power Converter System.

"The challenge is to meet customer expectations and specifications for complex energy storage systems. Numerous factors must be carefully considered, as they can impact both the system's price and capacity. In response to this specific request, Sparq Technology successfully addressed the challenge by constructing a 400 kW-430 kWh BESS integrated into a 10-foot container", Frédéric Coosemans, BESS specialist at Sparq.

BESS for a better tomorrow.

The BESS not only enhances reliability by providing backup power during outages but also significantly reduces energy costs during peak hours. This represents a highly cost-efficient investment for both small and large businesses seeking self-sufficiency and long-term business optimization. In a world increasingly focused on sustainability and energy efficiency, Sparq Technology's innovative solutions are making a meaningful difference.

"In addition to aligning with global climate objectives, energy storage promises to strengthen and secure our society as a whole. We must seek new solutions that allow individuals to become less reliant on external factors. It's crucial that we adopt a long-term perspective rather than fixate on the here and now", Stefan Rundéus, CEO at Cebon Group.

Curious about the potential of battery storage for your business? With over 35 years of industry experience in Sweden, we're well-equipped to assist you in finding the perfect solution for your needs.

Our team of engineers and local representatives can tailor our batteries to meet your specific requirements, no matter the size of your project. We're here to support you and answer all your questions.
