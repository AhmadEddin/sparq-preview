Meet Sparq at Upphandlingsdagarna 2026!

A stable and secure electricity supply is a strategic priority for municipalities, regions, and private organizations.

As technology evolves, the energy landscape is shifting toward decentralized, resilient solutions. But why is this happening now, and what does it actually mean in practice?

Join Sparq and Siemens eMobility for a forward-looking discussion on "The Energy Landscape of the Future" during Upphandlingsdagarna. After the seminar, you'll find us together with other leading experts in energy and security solutions at the Cebon group booth.

Are you ready to take on the energy challenges of tomorrow?

  • Date: 22-23 January
  • Booth: K:40
  • Seminar: 14.15-15.00 Day two
